# 🔗 FUNNEL WORKFLOW

## PURPOSE
Convert attention into clicks, leads, and revenue.

This is where traffic becomes money.  
If this is weak, nothing else matters.

---

## CORE FLOW

Content → Bio Link → Link Page → Landing Page → Offer → Conversion

---

## FUNNEL STRUCTURE

### 1. CONTENT (Traffic Source)

**Goal:**
- Stop attention
- Create curiosity
- Drive click intent

**Structure:**
Hook → Problem → Insight → CTA

**CTA Examples:**
- "Check what you qualify for — link in bio"
- "See your real options — link in bio"

---

### 2. BIO LINK (Entry Point)

**Goal:**
Direct user into funnel with zero confusion

**Rules:**
- ONE clear action only
- No multiple options

**Example:**
Fix Your Credit — See Your Options Below

---

### 3. LINK PAGE (Optional Layer)

**Goal:**
Bridge between platform and landing page

**Rules:**
- Only ONE primary button
- Remove distractions

**Example Button:**
Check Your Credit Options

---

### 4. LANDING PAGE (Conversion Layer)

**Goal:**
- Capture attention
- Build urgency
- Drive action

**Structure:**

**Headline**  
Call out the user directly  
Example:  
Struggling with a Low Credit Score?

**Problem Section**  
Highlight pain  
Example:  
Most people are stuck below 600 because they’re doing this wrong.

**Insight Section**  
Introduce curiosity  
Example:  
There are ways to improve your score without hurting it — but most people never check this first.

**CTA Section**  
Clear action  
Example:  
See What You Qualify For Now

---

## MONETIZATION PATHS

### OPTION A — AFFILIATE

User clicks → redirected to offer → payout generated

**Examples:**
- Credit monitoring tools
- Loan matching services
- Credit repair programs

---

### OPTION B — LEAD GENERATION

User submits info → stored → routed to partner

**Captured Data:**
- Name
- Email
- Phone (optional)

---

## FUNNEL RULES

- One goal per funnel
- One audience per funnel
- One offer per funnel
- No distractions
- No unnecessary pages

---

## COMMON MISTAKES

- Too many links
- Weak CTA
- Overcomplicated landing page
- No clear outcome
- Trying to monetize multiple ways at once

---

## PERFORMANCE METRICS

Track:
- Click Through Rate (CTR)
- Landing Page Conversion Rate
- Cost per Lead (if applicable)
- Revenue per Click

---

## OPTIMIZATION

If clicks are low:
- Fix content CTA

If clicks are high but no conversions:
- Fix landing page

If conversions happen but revenue is low:
- Change offer

---

## OBJECTIVE

Create a simple, direct path from attention to action that consistently converts traffic into revenue.
